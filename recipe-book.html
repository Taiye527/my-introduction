Jollof Rice

Details

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 45 minutes
  • Servings: 6

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Rice 4 cups
Tomato Paste 3 tbsp
Onions 2

Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly.
  2. Blend tomatoes and onions.
  3. Cook the sauce and add seasonings.
  4. Add rice and cook until tender.

Pancakes

Details

  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Flour 2 cups
Milk 1½ cups
Eggs 2

Steps

  1. Mix flour, milk, and eggs.
  2. Whisk until smooth.
  3. Pour batter onto a hot pan.
  4. Cook both sides until golden brown.

Source: AllRecipes
